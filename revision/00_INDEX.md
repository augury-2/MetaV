## Standing issues identified in initial read (not yet raised by reviewers)

Retained here so they can be folded into whichever comment provides the opening.

1. **No governing theoretical lens.** Institutional / stakeholder / absorptive-capacity theory appear only
   as one-line justifications inside individual hypotheses. Recommended remedy: NRBV + institutional
   theory dual lens, with resource orchestration explaining the sequencing.
2. **H1-H8 carry zero citations** and no causal mechanisms or boundary conditions.
3. **Economy-classification inconsistency.** Title/abstract/§2.4 say "developing vs emerging"; §3.2 says
   "developed and emerging"; §4.1 mixes both. No operational criterion (IMF/World Bank/MSCI) and no
   country list.
4. **Missing PLS-SEM protocol:** MICOM invariance before MGA; indirect/mediation effects; f2; Q2;
   PLSpredict; SRMR; full collinearity VIF.
5. **fsQCA gaps:** causal asymmetry claimed but never tested (no ~GLP analysis); no predictive validity
   test; consistency threshold stated as 0.80 in §3.5 but 0.82 in the §4.5 narrative.
6. **Cross-reference errors:** Introduction road map points to §3/4/5 for framework/method/results, but the
   framework sits at 2.5, method at 3, results at 4. §2.5 says the structural model appears "in section 5"
   (it is in 4.3). ("Section 9" in §3.1 fixed under R1-C01.)
7. **Vignettes (§4.1a)** present quoted interview data with no qualitative protocol, sampling rationale, or
   ethics statement, while §3.1 describes the design as quantitative.
8. **Sampling reporting:** no response rate, no survey window, no non-response bias test, no ethics
   approval, no country list.
9. **Reference base:** ~90% pre-2022, no DOIs. Two apparent misdatings — Khan et al. listed as 2022 but
   volume 30(8) of BSE is 2021; Liu et al. listed as 2020 but IJPDLM 48(8) is 2018.
10. **Vague reporting:** R2 for GLP given as "the 0.54 to 0.62 range across specifications".
