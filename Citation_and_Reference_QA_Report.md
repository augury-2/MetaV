## 2. The critical finding

**The submitted file contained no reference list.** Section 1.11 promises "the consolidated
references" at the end of the volume, but the document ended at Section 8.6 (Directions for Future
Research). Every one of the 837 in-text citations was therefore an orphan. Building and verifying a
complete APA 7 reference list was consequently the largest single element of this audit.

A second finding emerged during verification: **28 cited works could not be traced to any real
publication.** Each was checked against Crossref DOI metadata, publisher records, institutional
repositories and the wider literature. Where a claim was genuine but the attributed source was not,
the citation was replaced with a verified source that supports the same statement; nothing was
invented to fill a gap.

## 7. APA 7 formatting corrections applied

1. **Ampersand rule.** 153 parenthetical citations converted from `and` to `&`. Narrative citations correctly retain `and`.
2. **Alphabetical ordering within parentheses.** All 153 multi-source groups re-ordered by first author surname, as APA 7 requires.
3. **Duplicate suppression.** Repeated sources within a single parenthetical removed (e.g. `Tulachan, 2001; Tulachan, 2001`).
4. **Same-author year merging.** Works by an identical author list merged into one element (`Negi & Anand, 2014, 2015`; `Joshi et al., 2009, 2010`; `Barney, 1991, 1995`). Deliberately *not* applied where the author lists differ (`Kahraman et al., 2003` vs `Kahraman et al., 2015`; `Sharma et al., 2022` vs `Sharma et al., 2023`), since merging there would misrepresent authorship.
5. **`et al.` from first use.** Seven long author strings shortened.
6. **Same-year, same-first-author disambiguation.** The two distinct Murthy 2009 studies are distinguished by additional surnames.
7. **Lowercase particle.** `Van Laarhoven` → `van Laarhoven`; the truncated narrative form corrected.
8. **Group authors and abbreviations.** `MoAandFW` → `MoA&FW`, introduced in full at first use and used consistently thereafter; the reference is filed under *Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare* to match the in-text form.
9. **Software citation.** `The jamovi project (2023)`, with version and medium.
10. **Missing punctuation.** `Webster and Watson 2002` → `Webster & Watson, 2002`.
11. **Reference list.** Strict alphabetical order; hanging indent 0.5 in; double spacing; sentence-case article and book titles; title-case journal names; journal name and volume italicised; issue number roman in parentheses; en-dashes in page ranges; DOIs as `https://doi.org/...`; editions, publishers, repository handles and software versions supplied.
